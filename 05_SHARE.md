
# 05 — SHARE: Customer Retention Dashboard

## 1. Sharing the Results

The results of the customer retention analysis were presented through an interactive dashboard created using Tableau Public.

The dashboard provides a visual overview of customer cancellations, churn rates, membership plans, and training frequency.

## 2. Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes three visualizations:

### Customer Cancellations by Membership Plan
Shows the number of cancelled customers across Basic, Standard, and Premium membership plans.

### Churn Rate by Membership Plan
Compares the percentage of cancellations across different membership plans.

### Churn by Training Frequency
Shows the relationship between weekly training frequency and customer churn rates.

## 3. Key Insights Communicated

- The overall customer churn rate was 15.2%.
- The Basic membership plan had the highest churn rate (18.64%).
- The Premium membership plan had the lowest churn rate (7.07%).
- Customers training five times per week had the lowest observed churn rate (2.78%).
- Customers training once per week had the highest observed churn rate (19.00%).

## 5. Communication and Limitations

The dashboard was designed to communicate the findings clearly and support data-informed business discussions.

The analysis identifies associations between training frequency, membership plans, and customer churn. It does not establish causal relationships.
