
# 06 — ACT: Business Recommendations and Action Plan

## 1. Objective

The objective of this phase is to translate the customer retention analysis findings into practical business actions that could help reduce customer churn.

## 2. Recommended Actions

### 1. Improve Basic Membership Retention

The Basic membership plan presented the highest churn rate (18.64%).

Recommended actions:
- Conduct regular customer satisfaction check-ins.
- Review the benefits included in the Basic plan.
- Offer personalized engagement initiatives.
- Identify customers at risk of cancellation.

### 2. Encourage Consistent Training Habits

Customers with higher training frequency showed lower observed churn rates.

Recommended actions:
- Create weekly attendance challenges.
- Encourage realistic and consistent training routines.
- Track attendance patterns.
- Provide personalized support to less active customers.

### 3. Monitor Customer Engagement

Attendance rate and training frequency can help identify customers who may need additional support.

Recommended actions:
- Monitor changes in attendance.
- Contact customers showing declining engagement.
- Offer support to help them maintain consistency.

## 3. Suggested Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The business could monitor the following indicators:

- Monthly customer churn rate.
- Customer retention rate.
- Weekly training attendance.
- Churn rate by membership plan.
- Customer engagement levels.

## 4. Expected Business Impact

Implementing these recommendations could help the fitness business:

- Improve customer engagement.
- Identify potential cancellations earlier.
- Strengthen customer relationships.
- Support more informed retention decisions.

These are proposed actions and expected benefits, not measured outcomes from this dataset.

## 5. Final Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analysis can support business decision-making.

By combining SQL analysis, Google BigQuery, and Tableau Public, customer retention patterns were identified and translated into practical recommendations.

The analysis highlights the importance of monitoring customer engagement and developing targeted retention strategies.
