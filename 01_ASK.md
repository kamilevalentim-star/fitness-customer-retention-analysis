# 01 — ASK: Define the Business Problem

## Project Title
Fitness Business Analytics: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

A fictional fitness company is experiencing customer cancellations and wants to understand why customers are leaving.

The company needs a data-driven analysis to identify customer behavior patterns and opportunities to improve retention.

## 2. Project Objective

The main objective is to analyze customer data, identify factors associated with membership cancellations, and provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 3. Business Questions

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
2. Which membership plans have the highest cancellation rates?
3. Is training frequency associated with customer retention?
4. How long do customers typically stay with the company?
5. Which customer segments are more likely to cancel?
6. What strategies could help improve customer retention?

## 4. Stakeholders

- Business Manager
- Marketing Team
- Customer Success Team
- Finance Team

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Customer Churn Rate
- Customer Retention Rate
- Average Customer Lifetime
- Monthly Revenue
- Cancellation Rate by Membership Plan

## 6. Project Scope

- Business: Fitness & Personal Training Studio
- Dataset: Fictional customer data
- Number of customers: 500
- Analysis period: 12 months
- Main focus: Customer retention and cancellations

## 7. Expected Outcome

Identify patterns associated with customer cancellations and provide actionable recommendations to help the company improve customer retention and business performance.
